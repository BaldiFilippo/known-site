About Known

In one line. Known is the food diary you can trust: every number says where it comes from.

Short. Known is a food diary for iPhone. You say, type, scan or photograph what you eat and Known records the values, marking each one: from the label, to confirm, or estimated by the most advanced AI models. You choose whether calories, protein, carbs or fat lead; everything is counted.

Long.

Known is a food diary for iPhone built around one idea: you should always know how sure a number is. Every line of the diary carries one of three marks. A full blue dot means the values come from the label, the brand's own numbers. Half a blue dot means they were found in a product database and are still to confirm: match them with the pack and the dot fills. A grey ring means the value is an estimate by the most advanced AI models, used only where no label exists.

Logging a meal takes the shape of the moment: say it out loud, type a line, scan a barcode or a nutrition label, or take a photo of the plate. Known always counts calories, protein, carbs and fat; you choose which one leads in the diary and the widgets.

Packaged products are the hard part of any food diary. Reliable nutrition data is scarce, and most apps lean on catalogs that are patchy and out of date. Known does not hide it: values nobody has checked yet carry half a dot. Each person who matches a product with its pack fills the dot, and once the check is reviewed the product is verified for everyone who scans it next. For the people who use Known it is a shared challenge: check every food together, one at a time, until every dot is full.

Known is made in Italy by Filippo Baldi. It has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking; the diary lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.
