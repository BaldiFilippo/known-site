Known – press kit
https://tryknown.app · hello@tryknown.app

Known is a food diary for iPhone. Every number says where it comes from:
the label (full blue dot), a product database still to confirm (half blue
dot), or an estimate by the most advanced AI models (grey ring).

Files
- known-wordmark-black.svg / .png, known-wordmark-white.svg / .png: the wordmark, always lowercase. Do not retype it in another font.
- known-app-icon-light-1024.png, known-app-icon-dark-1024.png: the app icon. Do not recolour, rotate or add effects.
- known-screenshot-1…6.png: App Store screenshots, 1320×2868, dark.
- known-cover-1200x630.png: cover image for articles and link previews.

Please keep the wordmark and icon on plain backgrounds, with clear space around them at least the height of the letter k. The name is written "Known" in text and "known" in the wordmark only.
