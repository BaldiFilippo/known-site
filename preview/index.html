Questions? Answers.

Who is Known for?

People who already keep track of what they eat, or want to start doing it properly: for training, for a calorie budget, for a low carb diet, or for their own reasons. If you want numbers you can rely on and an app that stays out of the way, it is for you.

Does Known tell me what to eat?

No. Known is a diary. You set your goals, and it shows you where you are against them. It is not a meal plan and it is not medical advice.

What do the three marks mean?

A full blue dot: values from the label, the brand's own numbers. Half a blue dot: to confirm, values found in a product database and not yet checked; match them with the pack and the dot fills. A grey ring: estimated by the most advanced AI models, used only where no label exists.

How does a food become verified?

Someone checks it against the pack: a label scan, or Values match when the numbers already agree. Each check is reviewed, then the product is verified for everyone. Only the barcode, the values and your country are sent, never your diary.

Which languages does Known speak?

English, Italian, French, German and Spanish for the app. For food, you can speak and write in any language your iPhone understands.

Where is my diary stored?

On your iPhone and in your iCloud. There is no Known account. Read the privacy policy for the details.
